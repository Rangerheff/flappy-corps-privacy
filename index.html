Privacy Policy for Flappy Corps

App package: com.flappycorps
Effective date: 23 September 2026
Developer / publisher: Corrie Heffner
Contact: chranger101@gmail.com

1. Overview

Flappy Corps (“the App”) is a free, offline-capable skill game distributed as an Android application that wraps a local HTML5/WebView experience. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App does and does not handle.

2. Who we are

The App is published by Corrie Heffner. For privacy questions, contact: chranger101@gmail.com.

3. No accounts

The App does not require an account, login, or registration. We do not operate a user profile system for Flappy Corps.

4. No sale of data

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share personal information with data brokers.

5. Advertising and analytics

The current Play build of Flappy Corps is not designed to show third-party advertisements and does not integrate third-party analytics, crash-reporting, or advertising SDKs (for example, no Firebase Analytics, AdMob, or similar services were included in the reviewed project).

If ads or analytics are added in a future version, this policy will be updated before or when that version is published, and Play Console Data safety answers will be revised.

6. Information the App may keep on your device

Gameplay progress such as a high score or settings may be stored locally on your device (for example via WebView local storage or Android app preferences). That information stays on your device unless you clear the App’s data or uninstall the App. We do not receive a copy of those local scores on our servers because the App does not include a cloud save or account backend.

7. Permissions

The Android package may declare the following permissions:

  • INTERNET — Declared for the Android WebView / platform stack. Game content for this build is packaged with the App for local play. The permission does not mean we collect personal data over the network.
  • VIBRATE — Used for optional haptic feedback during gameplay. This does not transmit personal data.

The App does not request access to your contacts, microphone, camera, precise location, or files for its core gameplay.

8. Children

The App is a general-audience skill game. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because we do not collect personal information through the App, there is no child personal data for us to delete from our servers. Parents may uninstall the App or clear App data on the device at any time.

9. Third-party services

Distribution via Google Play is subject to Google’s terms and privacy policy. We do not control Google Play’s processing of install or store-account data.

10. Data security

Because core gameplay data remains on your device and we do not run an App-specific user data backend for Flappy Corps, there is no developer cloud database of Flappy Corps player profiles to breach. You should still keep your device updated and use standard device security practices.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Effective date” at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the App after an update means you acknowledge the revised policy.
